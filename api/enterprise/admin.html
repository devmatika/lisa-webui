Guide

How to work with Lisa.

For everyone

  1. Sign in with the link you received by e-mail or from your admin, and choose a password. Forgot it? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.
  2. Chat — click Back to chat. Your assistant is private: your conversations, memory and files are never visible to colleagues or administrators.
  3. Telegram (optional) — under My account, link your Telegram with the pairing code the company bot sends you. Then you can talk to Lisa from your phone.
  4. Your accounts — if your role allows it, connect Google (mail, calendar, docs) under My account. ClickUp usually works without setup: Lisa finds the tasks assigned to your e-mail address.
  5. Company knowledge — ask Lisa "how do we …?". She answers from the areas you may read and tells you when she does not know. Under Knowledge you can search yourself and Teach Lisa: your suggestion is published after an approver checks it.

Good to know

  • Lisa remembers things you tell her ("remember that …"); you can review that memory in the chat settings.
  • Before Lisa sends an e-mail or creates something in another system, she asks you to confirm.
  • Missing something? Ask your administrator — your role decides which features you see.

For administrators

  1. Run the setup wizard (button at the top) once: company, AI model for everyone, company tools (Telegram bot, Google, Microsoft 365, ClickUp), company knowledge and your team. Press Apply at the end: every assistant is refreshed and Lisa is back within about 20 seconds.
  2. Add people under Users with a role. Send them the password link (or it is e-mailed automatically when e-mail is configured).
  3. Adjust roles under Roles: what each role's assistant may do and which company tools it may use. The built-in roles are a good start: Staff for most people, Manager to approve knowledge, Administrator for people management, Operator (IT) for the setup wizard, AI model and server settings.
  4. Connect the company vault under Knowledge → Company vault: paste the Git address of the Obsidian vault, give Lisa read access (her key as a GitLab/GitHub deploy key, or a read-only token), then tick per folder who may read it — e.g. Knowledge for everyone, Projects for managers only. Lisa updates it by herself every hour; nobody needs their own Obsidian.
  5. Watch Status & audit log: failed logins, denied requests and every change are recorded.

How shared tools work

  • Telegram: one company bot; each person links their own Telegram and gets their own private chat.
  • Google: the company provides the sign-in app; each person connects their own Google account.
  • Microsoft 365 / Outlook: one company app; each assistant reaches only its owner's mailbox.
  • ClickUp: connected once with a company token; each person sees the tasks assigned to their e-mail. A personal token is optional.
  • Knowledge: one company vault, split into areas with their own access.

Troubleshooting

  • Someone lost access — send a new password link from Users. Disabling a person signs them out everywhere at once.
  • The setup wizard or AI settings are missing — your account needs the Operator (IT) role. Lisa refuses changes that would leave nobody able to manage users or run setup. If you are locked out anyway, on the server run:
    docker compose exec webui /app/venv/bin/python -m api.enterprise.recover you@company.com
  • Lisa does not answer — check Status: the gateway must be "ok". After the wizard's Apply it restarts for about 20 seconds.
  • The vault shows "Not synced" — the message under it says why: usually Lisa's deploy key was not added to the repository, or the access token expired. Fix it and press Sync now.
